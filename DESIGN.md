# Zoomies Visual System

## Thesis

Zoomies should feel like a Colombo movement poster that came alive, not a generic run club site.

## Palette

- Chestnut: `#480903` for dark cinematic surfaces.
- Olive: `#937A24` for utility marks and low-frequency contrast.
- Desert Tan: `#E5D1A4` for warm readable surfaces.
- Caramel: `#CE793A` for transitional warmth.
- Copper: `#B64B12` for route and poster energy.
- Logo Red: `#D90909` for activation and the draft mark.

## Form

Sharp rectangles, cropped media, route lines, poster frames, oversized kinetic typography, and controlled disorder. Avoid glassy SaaS panels, rounded card stacks, generic fitness photography, and purple/blue tech gradients.

## Motion

Motion is directional and physical: text decodes, route lines draw, cards stack, poster layers stretch, and CTA clicks ripple. Use Anime.js where available, with CSS fallbacks and reduced-motion support.

On mobile, motion supports speed without stealing the viewport: no cursor-following effects, lighter texture, visible content defaults when Anime.js fails, shorter scroll reveals, and a persistent bottom RSVP action.

## Type

Display type must feel heavy, condensed, and poster-like. Body copy must stay readable and direct. Monospace is reserved for time, distance, event data, and small operational labels.

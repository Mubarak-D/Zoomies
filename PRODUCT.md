# Zoomies Product Truth

Zoomies is a Colombo-based social running crew.

The product is not running performance. The product is showing up: movement, games, challenges, people, and Colombo energy.

Primary site job: make a first-time visitor think "I want to be part of this" and make joining the next event easy.

Launch scope:
- Home landing page
- What Zoomies is
- What happens at a Zoomies
- Next event
- First-timer reassurance
- Instagram CTA
- Lightweight RSVP

Do not build before launch:
- Authentication
- Member dashboards
- Leaderboards
- Strava clones
- Payments
- Complex CMS

Core line: More than a run.

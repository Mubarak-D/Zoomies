No second thought

Stuff people ask before showing up.

Do I need to be fast?

No. The launch format is social pace. If a future event is faster, it will be labelled clearly.

Can I come alone?

Yes. The event is designed so first-timers are noticed and folded in.

What do I bring?

Comfortable shoes, water, and enough curiosity to follow instructions at a checkpoint.

Where do we meet?

The exact location is sent after RSVP so people do not have to hunt for details.
